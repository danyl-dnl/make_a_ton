# SubscriptIQ
### Subscription Management and Recurring Billing System

SubscriptIQ is a unified platform designed to simplify and automate the complete subscription lifecycle, including product management, recurring billing, invoicing, payment tracking, and revenue monitoring.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **User Management:** Role-based access control (RBAC) for Admin, Staff, and Customers.
- **Product & Plan Management:** Flexible catalog management for products, variants, recurring intervals (monthly, yearly), and custom tiers.
- **Subscription Lifecycle:** Create, activate, pause, resume, upgrade, downgrade, and cancel subscriptions seamlessly.
- **Quotation Management:** Generate, send, and convert quotations into active subscriptions.
- **Automated Billing & Invoicing:** Automated recurring invoice generation with tax calculations and multi-currency readiness.
- **Discount & Coupon Engine:** Configure and apply fixed-amount, percentage-based, and duration-limited discounts.
- **Payment & Receivables Tracking:** Real-time visibility into paid, pending, failed, and overdue invoices with retry mechanisms.
- **Subscription Health & Retention:** Proactive monitoring for upcoming renewals, payment failures, and churn risk.
- **Analytics & Reporting:** Interactive dashboards tracking MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue), ARR, customer lifetime value, and active subscriber metrics.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience & Use Cases

- **SaaS Businesses:** Manage tiered software subscriptions, usage add-ons, and automated renewals.
- **Service Providers:** Streamline periodic retainer billing and service contract management.
- **Digital Content & Media:** Handle recurring memberships and gated access models.
