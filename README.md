# SubscriptIQ
### Subscription Management and Recurring Billing System

SubscriptIQ is a unified platform designed to simplify and automate the complete subscription lifecycle, including product management, recurring billing, invoicing, payment tracking and revenue monitoring.

## Key Features

- **User Management:** Role-based access for Admin, Staff and Customers.
- **Product and Plan Management:** Manage products, variants and subscription plans.
- **Subscription Management:** Create, track, renew, pause and close subscriptions.
- **Quotation Management:** Create and manage customer quotations.
- **Automated Billing:** Generate invoices with automatic tax calculations.
- **Discount Management:** Configure and apply fixed and percentage-based discounts.
- **Payment Tracking:** Monitor payments, outstanding balances and overdue invoices.
- **Analytics Dashboard:** Monitor revenue, active subscriptions and financial performance.
- **Subscription Health Monitoring:** Track upcoming renewals and expiring subscriptions.

## Objective

To provide businesses with a centralised and reliable solution for managing subscriptions, automating recurring billing and gaining better visibility into their financial operations.
